Frequently Asked Questions

How does AI recipe generation work?

You answer a short guided sequence — region, cuisine, dish, servings, budget, dietary needs, ingredients, cooking method, time, and difficulty. AI Recipe Agent sends those preferences to GPT-4.1-mini through a dedicated recipe-planning agent, which returns a complete, structured recipe with ingredients, step-by-step instructions, nutrition, tips, and substitutions.

Can I change servings?

Yes. Every recipe page has a servings scaler that recalculates ingredient quantities automatically. You can also ask the AI to adapt an existing recipe to a specific new serving size.

Can I use ingredients I already have?

Yes — in the AI Recipe Builder you can list ingredients on hand, and the generator will try to build the recipe around them. On any recipe page, you can also use "Adapt Recipe → Use what I have" to minimize hard-to-find ingredients.

Can I save recipes?

Yes. Since there's no login required, saved recipes are stored in your browser's local storage and available on the Saved Recipes page on this device.

Can I download recipes?

Yes. Every recipe page has a "Download / Print PDF" button that opens a print-optimized version of the recipe (title, description, servings, timings, ingredients, instructions, tips, and nutrition) which you can save as a PDF from your browser's print dialog.

How are recipes personalized?

Within your current session, we track the cuisines, dietary needs, budgets, and time constraints you engage with to power the "Recommended For You" section. This is session-based personalization, not long-term account tracking — there's no login system.
